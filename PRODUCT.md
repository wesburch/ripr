# RIPR

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Terminal CLI with a persistent TUI. The first deliverable is a browser-based interactive design study, not a working downloader.

## Product Purpose

Paste a video URL, choose an audio format and destination folder, and extract audio without leaving the terminal interface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The requested design includes URL entry, output format, destination, progress, recent rips, ASCII branding, and a reaper mascot. The user confirmed that the tool takes priority and any mascot should stay subtle. They also explicitly gave freedom to omit or reinvent the mascot. Progress animation may briefly show the reaper slicing video to release audio.

## Brand Commitments

Premium, creative terminal presentation. Working spelling is RIPR, matching the project directory; final spelling remains open because the spoken brief also spelled RRIPR.

## Evidence on Hand

Greenfield project. All jobs, progress, filenames, and metadata in the design study are illustrative. No media extraction or filesystem operations are implemented.

## Open Decisions

Implementation language, terminal framework, supported platforms and URL providers, extraction engine, and supported codec settings remain undecided. MP3, WAV, FLAC, Opus, and AAC are proposed design options.
